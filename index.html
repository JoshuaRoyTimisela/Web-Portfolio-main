My Projects

FoodDelivery SPK

A web-based decision support system for selecting the best online food delivery service using the Simple Additive Weighting (SAW) method, based on key criteria such as price, promotions, delivery costs, speed, merchant availability, and ease of use.

MariBayar

A web-based electricity payment information system designed to simplify postpaid electricity management. The system allows users to input electricity usage, view their bills, and manage payments in a simple and transparent way.

Python Discount Calculator

A simple Python-based calculator designed to calculate the final price of products after applying discounts. The application allows users to enter the original price, discount percentage, and quantity to calculate the total price easily and accurately.
